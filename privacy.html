Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

OptimizeMe reads your health data to show it back to you and to generate wellness suggestions. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties for their own purposes.

What we handle

SourceData
Apple Health Only the categories you approve. OptimizeMe requests read access only and never requests permission to write to your Health record.
Google Health Sleep and sleep stages, heart rate, heart-rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, activity, workouts, body weight and body composition.
Strava Your activities: type, time, distance, pace, elevation, power and heart rate.
Oura, WHOOP Sleep, recovery, workouts and body measurements, if you connect them.
You Notes, supplements, water, meal timing, stress ratings and activity tags.

Where it lives

DataStoredLeaves your device?
Apple Health dataOn your device Only within a request you trigger, to compute your analysis
Your notes and manual logOn your device Sent with an analysis request, not retained afterwards
Connections to Google, Strava, Oura, WHOOP Encrypted with AES-256-GCM No
Analysis resultsHeld in memory for displayNo

Apple Health data is never written to iCloud, in line with Apple's requirement that HealthKit data may not be stored there.

Analysis requests are not persisted. When the app asks the server to analyse a day, the request carries that day's measurements and your manual log. The server computes a response and discards the input. There is no request-body logging and no analytics performed on health data.

Access tokens for connected services are encrypted at rest, with your account and the specific service bound into the encryption so a stored credential cannot be reused for a different account or service.

What OptimizeMe never does

  • Use health data for advertising or marketing
  • Sell health data or share it with data brokers
  • Build advertising profiles from health data
  • Share health data with third parties for their own purposes
  • Require an account for features that do not need one

Your control

  • Revoke Apple Health access at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Health, or in the Health app.
  • Disconnect any service at any time, which deletes its stored credentials.
  • Delete your account, which erases your stored data and all connected service credentials.
  • Delete the app to remove everything held on the device.

Not medical care

OptimizeMe provides general wellness information with citations to published research. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for professional medical care.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will change and the app will note it. Continued use after a change indicates acceptance.
