Your body, as an experiment you can actually read

OptimizeMe brings your wearable data together with the variables a tracker cannot see, then tells you what each number means and what to try next.

What it does

  • Combines sleep, heart rate, HRV, workouts and body composition from the services you already use.
  • Lets you log what the sensors miss: supplements, meal timing, water, alcohol, stress and screens.
  • Explains each measurement against published reference ranges and against your own baseline, rather than showing a number and leaving you to guess.
  • Suggests small, concrete experiments, each with the research it rests on.

Connected sources

Apple Health, Google Health (including Fitbit devices) and Strava, with Oura and WHOOP in progress. Where two services describe the same workout, each contributes what it measures best.

Not medical care. OptimizeMe provides general wellness information based on published research. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Talk to a clinician about symptoms or before changing treatment.

Privacy in one line

Your health data stays on your device. Analysis requests are computed and discarded, never stored, and your data is never used for advertising or sold to anyone. The privacy policy spells out exactly what is handled and where it lives.
